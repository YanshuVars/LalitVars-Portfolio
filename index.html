01  Profile

Supply chains are relationships before they are contracts.

I work where engineering meets commerce — developing vendors, driving cost leadership, and industrialising new products without compromising quality.

My career began on the shop floor in manufacturing engineering at RICO Auto, moved through vendor development at Honda and Tata Motors, then into strategic sourcing leadership at Eicher Engines (TAFE) — and today I lead commodity sourcing for proprietary parts at Mahindra's Swaraj Division in Mohali.

The through-line across two-wheelers, passenger vehicles, engines and tractors is simple: dependable vendors, competitive costs, and products delivered on time. I speak the language of the shop floor and the language of the negotiation table — and that is where the best sourcing decisions get made.

  • a. End-to-end ownership — strategy, sourcing, vendor development, launch
  • b. Deep domain in engine & mechanical proprietary components
  • c. Cost reduction without quality compromise
  • d. QMS implementation across OEM and supplier operations
